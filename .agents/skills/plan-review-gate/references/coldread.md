First write a paraphrase under two headings, "What this proposes" and "How the existing system works, per this document", in two to five sentences each.
A paraphrase that comes out wrong or impossible is itself a finding.
Then flag undefined project terms, unstated prerequisites, vague steps, and places where parts of the plan do not connect even though every term is defined.
